This assignment has three broken scenarios. Each produces a different error. Diagnose each one before fixing.

Scenario 1 — No role attached:

Scenario 3 — Right permissions, wrong resource: 6. Update the permissions policy to include s3:GetObject but scope it to a different bucket ARN (not the one you're trying to access). 7. Try to download again. What happens? Same error as Scenario 2, or different?

🔧 Assignment 4B — S3 Bucket Policy (Resource-Based Policy)
Bucket policy examples: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/userguide/example-bucket-policies.html

Create an S3 bucket. Block all public access.
Create an IAM user with no S3 permissions in their identity-based policy.
Try to list the bucket as that user. Expected: denied.
Add a bucket policy that allows s3:ListBucket and s3:GetObject for that specific user's ARN. Try again.
Now add an explicit deny for that user in the bucket policy. Also add an explicit allow for them in their identity-based policy. Try to access the bucket. What wins?
Question for your log: Walk through the cross-account policy evaluation logic (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/IAM/latest/UserGuide/reference_policies_evaluation-logic-cross-account.html). Why does explicit deny in the resource policy win even when the identity policy says allow?

🔧 Assignment 4C — Cross-Account S3 Access (Pair Assignment)
Pair with another fresher. One of you is Account A (S3 bucket owner), the other is Account B (EC2 that needs access).

Goal: EC2 in Account B reads objects from a bucket in Account A. No hardcoded credentials anywhere.

Account A:

Create an S3 bucket. Upload a few files.
Create an IAM role cross-account-s3-role:
Trust policy: allows Account B's account ID (not a specific user — the account) to assume this role
Permissions policy: s3:GetObject and s3:ListBucket on your bucket ARN specifically
Add a bucket policy allowing the cross-account-s3-role ARN to access the bucket.
Why are both the role AND the bucket policy needed for cross-account? Read the cross-account evaluation docs and explain before proceeding.

Account B:

Create an IAM role for EC2 with permission to call sts:AssumeRole on Account A's role ARN specifically.
Launch an EC2 with this role attached.
SSH in. Assume Account A's role:
aws sts assume-role \

Export the temporary credentials as environment variables. List and download files from Account A's bucket.
Questions for your log:
