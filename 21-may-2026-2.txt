🔧 Assignment 3E — EFS: Shared Filesystem
Mounting EFS: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/efs/latest/ug/mounting-fs.html EFS access points: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/efs/latest/ug/efs-access-points.html

⚠️ EFS is not free tier. You will be charged ~$0.30/GB-month prorated to the time you use it. Keep this exercise under 1 hour and delete the filesystem immediately when done. Total cost: a few cents.

Create an EFS filesystem. Create mount targets in both Public Subnet A and Public Subnet B. (EFS needs a Security Group that allows NFS port 2049 inbound from your EC2 instances.)
Launch two EC2 instances — one in each public subnet.
Mount the EFS filesystem on both:
sudo mkdir /shared
sudo mount -t efs <fs-id>:/ /shared
On Instance 1: echo "written from instance 1" | sudo tee /shared/test.txt
On Instance 2: cat /shared/test.txt — is the file there?
Delete the EFS filesystem immediately after verifying.
Question for your log: EBS vs EFS — when would you use each? Can two EC2 instances in different AZs share the same EBS volume? Can they share the same EFS filesystem? What does that tell you about the architectural difference?
